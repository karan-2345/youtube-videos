# Beat plan v1: the pistol shrimp's snap (1080 x 1920, 60 fps, about 35 s)

**Look: "Cut-away"**, the third Short in the series: same dark warm backdrop, type, captions, label pills and
cinnabar accent. The real thing first, then the claw cut open. This one is underwater, so the new colours are sea
blue-green, sand, coral and the shrimp's orange-red. The shrimp is our own stylised design, not anyone else's.

New actors:
- **Pistol shrimp:** a segmented body, carapace, tail fan, antennae, walking legs, a small claw and one oversized
  snapping claw with a plunger on the moving finger and a socket in the fixed one.
- **Fish:** a small goby-like fish that can be stunned (it stops, rolls and drifts).
- **Seabed:** sand, a burrow, a few rocks, floating particles and caustic light.
- **Cavitation bubble:** grows in the jet, then collapses to a point with a shock-wave ring and a flash.
- **Sonar:** a submarine above the reef, a ping, and a sonar screen that fills with noise.

Sound: underwater ambience, the cocking click, a sharp snap with a bubble pop, a deep crack for the collapse, a
bright zing for the flash, a sonar ping smothered in crackle. Music: same family as the other two Shorts.
