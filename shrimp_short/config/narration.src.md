# Narration source: the pistol shrimp's snap (vertical Short, 30-40 s)

> Edit this file, then `python build.py narration`. One line = one beat (a paragraph; 0.8 s after it).
> Sentences inside a beat get 0.4 s. `@pause N` adds silence after the previous beat. `@lead N` adds a picture-only
> moment before the next beat. Every number is a {{fact}} token (this script speaks none; "World War Two" is a name).
> v1 (2026-11-28), waiting for approval. 98 words, 5 beats, about 35 s (estimated from the wrinkle Short's measured
> rate; measure with `build.py tts`).
> Rules carried over: the hook lands in the first sentence; every sentence starts with a connective or an explicit
> link to the one before; no fragments, colon reveals or mic-drop kickers.
> Spine: it knocks out fish without touching them -> the claw's water jet tears open a bubble -> the bubble's
> collapse is the crack that stuns the fish -> the collapse even flashes, nearly as hot as the Sun's surface ->
> a whole colony is loud enough to drown out sonar (World War Two).

## S00 · A claw that never touches
@scene S00_Hook
@lead 0.4
The pistol shrimp knocks out fish with a claw that never even touches them.

## S01 · The jet tears open a bubble
@scene S01_Jet
Instead, it snaps the claw so fast that the jet of water it shoots out tears open a bubble.

## S02 · The crack
@scene S02_Crack
Then the bubble collapses with a crack loud enough to stun or even kill a small fish.

## S03 · As hot as the Sun
@scene S03_Flash
And as it collapses, it even flashes with light, which means that for an instant it's nearly as hot as the surface of the Sun.

## S04 · Louder than sonar
@scene S04_Sonar
So when thousands of these shrimp snap at once, their crackle can drown out navy sonar, which really happened in World War Two.
@pause 1.0
