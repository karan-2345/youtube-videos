S00.01 When you film a spinning propeller on your phone, the blades bend like bananas and some of them float away from the hub, even though the propeller is perfectly straight, and that's because your phone doesn't take a picture the way you think it does.

S01.01 Because you'd expect a photo to catch one single instant, but most phone cameras read the picture one row at a time, starting at the top of the sensor and sweeping down to the bottom.
S01.02 That sweep only takes a small fraction of a second, but it does mean the top of your photo is slightly older than the bottom.
