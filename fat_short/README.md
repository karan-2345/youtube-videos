# Where does the fat go when you lose weight? (vertical Short, 64.5 s)

"When you lose weight, the fat doesn't melt away or turn into muscle, and you don't sweat it out either, because most
of it leaves your body every time you breathe out." A glowing lump of fat busts three myths, then leaves a glass body
as golden specks through the lungs; one real fat molecule (C55H104O6) is broken apart by 78 O2 into 55 CO2 and 52 H2O,
atom for atom; a 10 kg block dissolves into 8.4 kg of breath and 1.6 kg of water; panting does nothing, jogging does.
