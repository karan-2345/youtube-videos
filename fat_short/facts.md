# facts.md: every claim in the "where does the fat go" Short

| | Claim | Source / check |
|---|---|---|
| PASS | the fat doesn't melt away or turn into muscle, and you don't sweat it out either | [M]: fat is oxidised to CO2 and water; it cannot become muscle (different tissue, different molecules); the misconceptions listed in [U] include energy/heat, muscle and excretion through the colon |
| PASS | most of it leaves your body every time you breathe out | [M]: "the lungs are the primary excretory organ for weight loss" |
| PASS | scientists asked 150 doctors, dietitians and personal trainers ... only three got it right | [U]: 50 family doctors, 50 dietitians and 50 personal trainers in Australia; only 3 answered correctly |
| PASS | fat is made of just three kinds of atoms, carbon, hydrogen and oxygen | triglyceride C55H104O6 [M] |
| PASS | it breaks the fat apart with the oxygen you breathe in | C55H104O6 + 78 O2 -> 55 CO2 + 52 H2O (atoms balance: checked) [M] |
| PASS | the pieces join up again as carbon dioxide ... and as water | [M] |
| PASS | water leaves in your sweat, your tears, your pee and even your breath | [M]: the water "may be excreted in the urine, faeces, sweat, breath, tears or other bodily fluids" |
| PASS | 10 kg of fat: 8.4 kg leave through your lungs, 1.6 kg turn into water | [M][N]; our atom count gives 84.3% / 15.7% |
| PASS | breathing faster won't help ... panting on purpose just makes you dizzy | [M]: "breathing more than required ... hyperventilation ... dizziness, palpitations and loss of consciousness"; [R]: hyperventilating leads to hypocapnia and does not burn more fat |
| PASS | your body only burns more fat when it needs more energy, like when you move | [M]: replacing one hour of rest with moderate exercise such as jogging raises the metabolic rate about sevenfold and removes about an extra 40 g of carbon |
| PASS | a tiny bit of you is leaving every time you breathe out | [M]: at rest about 12 breaths a minute, each carrying 33 mg of CO2 (8.9 mg of carbon); about 200 g of carbon a day, a third of it during sleep |
| PASS | (shown) 29 kg of oxygen in, 28 kg of CO2 and 11 kg of water out for 10 kg of fat | [M][N] (mass balances to 39 kg) |
