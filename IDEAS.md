# IDEAS.md: Shorts not made yet

Proposed to the user on 2026-11-21 (made since: Pisa, the fat one, and the Ice King as `ice_short/`). Each has a flowing storytelling hook in the
house style; check every fact before writing the full script.

**The box that made everything cheap**
> "Almost everything you own crossed an ocean inside a plain metal box, and before a trucking man named Malcolm McLean
> came up with that box in 1956, loading a ship cost about 36 times more."
- 3D: a dock full of loose sacks and barrels turning into neatly stacked containers.

**How long is a billion?**
> "A million seconds from now is less than two weeks away, so you'd think a billion seconds can't be that much longer,
> but a billion seconds from now you'd be almost 32 years older."
- 3D: a clock and a calendar stretching out as the numbers grow.

**Why airplane windows are round**
> "Every airplane window you've ever looked out of has round corners, and that isn't for style, because in 1954 two
> brand-new jets broke apart in the sky, and the cracks started at a corner."
- 3D: pressure building on a square window until a crack spreads from the corner; the same test on a round window holds.

**Landmark ideas, "like the Leaning Tower of Pisa"** (proposed 2026-11-23; the user picked the pyramid water one, made
as `pyramid_short/`). Facts not checked yet:

**Venice stands on a forest**
> "Venice is standing on millions of tree trunks that were hammered into the mud more than a thousand years ago, and
> they should have rotted away long ago, but they're still holding the city up."
- Surprise: no oxygen in the mud, so the fungi that rot wood can't live there (one church, the Salute, ~1.1 million posts).
- 3D: the closest to Pisa: a Venetian church with the ground cut open (water, mud, a forest of posts).

**The Eiffel Tower was meant to come down**
> "The Eiffel Tower was only meant to stand for twenty years, and Paris was ready to take it down, until Gustave
> Eiffel turned it into the world's tallest radio antenna."
- 3D: the tower building itself in time-lapse, a countdown to 1909, radio waves from the top.

**The Statue of Liberty used to be brown**
> "The Statue of Liberty wasn't always green, because when she arrived in New York she was the shiny brown of a
> brand-new penny, and the green that covers her now is the very thing that keeps her standing."
- 3D: the copper statue turning green over a decades-long time-lapse of rain and sea air.

**The Sydney Opera House roof nobody could build**
> "The Sydney Opera House was meant to take four years and cost seven million dollars, but it took fourteen years and
> cost over a hundred million, because nobody on Earth knew how to build its roof."
- Surprise: every shell is cut from the same sphere (like peeling an orange); the architect quit and never saw it.
- 3D: the sails slicing out of one glowing sphere.

**Big Ben is kept on time with pennies**
> "The clock in Big Ben's tower is kept on time by a little stack of old pennies, because adding just one coin to its
> giant pendulum makes the clock gain almost half a second a day."
