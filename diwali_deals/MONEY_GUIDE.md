# Make money with this Diwali video: step by step

**Timing:** Dhanteras is **Fri 6 Nov 2026** and Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) is **Sun 8 Nov 2026**.
Most people buy decor and gifts in the next 4–5 days, so **post today**.

**Honest expectations:** Affiliate income comes from *sales*, not views. Your first video
might make ₹0, or it might make a few hundred rupees. Income grows when you post every day
and build followers. Nobody can promise you money, and anyone who does is lying.

---

## Step 1: Get your affiliate (referral) links (≈15 minutes)

I can't make the account for you. It needs **your** name, phone, PAN and bank details.

### Option A: EarnKaro (easiest, recommended to start)
1. Install the **EarnKaro** app (Play Store) or go to earnkaro.com and sign up free.
2. Inside the app, open Amazon / Flipkart / Myntra / Ajio etc., open any product page,
   and press **"Make Link"** (or paste the product URL into the "Make Profit Link" box).
3. You get a short link. Anyone who buys through it earns you a commission.
4. Minimum withdrawal is very low (reported as ₹10, check in the app), paid to your bank.

### Option B: Amazon Associates (direct, a bit more work)
1. Go to **affiliate-program.amazon.in** and sign up. You need a website *or* your
   YouTube/Instagram profile link.
2. Use the **SiteStripe** bar at the top of Amazon.in to "Get Link" for any product.
3. ⚠️ You must make a few qualifying sales soon after joining or the account is closed.
   Read the current rules in the signup page.

**Which products earn most?** Home decor, fashion, beauty and gifting usually pay a higher %
than mobiles/electronics (electronics often only ~1–4%). The 5 items in this video
(lights, diyas, rangoli, lantern, gift hampers) are all decor/gifting on purpose.

## Step 4: Post the video everywhere (same video, free reach)

File: `final/diwali_deals.mp4`. Captions/hashtags ready to copy are in `post_kit.md`.

1. **Instagram Reels**: best for shopping in India. Add trending audio *at low volume*

Best times to post: **12–2 pm and 7–10 pm**.

## Step 5: Keep it going (this is where the money is)

- Post **1 deal video a day** until Bhai Dooj (10 Nov). I can make more videos with
  different products; just ask ("make one for gifts under ₹500", "kurta deals", etc.).
- After Diwali, do the same for **wedding season**, **Black Friday (27 Nov)** and
  **Christmas/New Year sales**.
- Check your EarnKaro/Amazon dashboard every day to see which product sells, and make
  the next video about similar items.

## Rules you must follow (to avoid getting banned)

- **Always disclose** that these are affiliate links (ASCI rules in India). The video already
  shows "#ad affiliate links" in the corner; also write **#ad** in your caption.
- Don't spam links in random people's comments or DMs. Platforms ban that.
- Don't claim fake prices or "90% off" unless it's true on the day you post.
- Commission is earned only on real purchases. Never buy from your own link to "test" it
  repeatedly. Programs treat this as fraud.
- Income is taxable once it's large; keep a record.
