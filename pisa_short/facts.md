# facts.md: every claim in the Leaning Tower of Pisa Short

| | Claim | Source / check |
|---|---|---|
| PASS | should have fallen over more than 800 years ago, when it was only three floors tall | work stopped in 1178 at the third floor (848 years ago); [B]: without the pauses "the tower would have fallen over" (the foundations would have failed in bearing) |
| PASS | what saved it ... was a war | [W]: work halted for almost a century "as the Republic of Pisa was almost continually engaged in battles with Genoa, Lucca, and Florence"; [B] notes the reasons for the stoppages are not recorded with certainty. The script says Pisa went to war and the work stopped, and that the pause saved it. |
| PASS | In 1173, Pisa began building | [W][B] |
| PASS | on soft, wet clay | [W]: "soft ground composed mostly of clay, fine sand, and shells"; the soft marine clay layer is the Pancone clay [B] |
| PASS | a foundation just three meters deep | [W] "a mere three-metre foundation" (an engineering survey gives up to 5.5 m including the later walkway excavation) |
| PASS | by the third floor, one side was already sinking | [W]: began to sink after construction had progressed to the second floor (European count = third floor) in 1178 |
| PASS | the work stopped for almost a century | 1178 to 1272: 94 years |
| PASS | the tower's weight slowly squeezed water out of the clay and made it firmer | [B]: by 1272 "the strength of the clay had increased due to consolidation under the weight of the Tower" (consolidation = water squeezed out of the pores) |
| PASS | if they'd kept building, it would have collapsed | [B]: "had it continued much further, the foundations would have experienced a bearing capacity failure" |
| PASS | each new floor a little taller on one side to hide the lean | [W]: "the engineers built upper floors with one side taller than the other. Because of this, the tower is curved." |
| PASS | curved, like a banana | [S]: "banana shape" |
| PASS | By 1990 it was leaning so far that it had to close | [W][N]: closed 7 January 1990, leaning about 5.5 degrees |
| PASS | engineers pulled soil from under the high side | [B][N]: inclined drills removed small amounts of soil from beneath the north (raised) side, 1999-2001 |
| PASS | straightened it by almost half a meter | [W]: straightened by 45 cm (other sources: 44-50 cm) |
| PASS | at least four strong earthquakes | [U] |
| PASS | by keeping it from swaying with the shaking | [U]: the tower's height and stiffness with the soft soil "modify the vibrational characteristics ... in such a way that the tower does not resonate with earthquake ground motion" (2019 paper: natural period 0.35 s -> over 1 s) |
| PASS | the bell chamber was added in 1372 (shown, not said) | [W] |
