# Narration source: hidden in plain sight (vertical Short) — DRAFT v1 for STOP 1

> Same format and rules as the chess Short: one line = one beat; the hook lands in the first sentence; every
> sentence starts with a connective or an explicit link to the one before; no fragments, colon reveals or mic-drop
> kickers; one number per idea; no decimal is ever spoken. Facts and FLAGs: research/notes.md.
> Spine: the arrow you've seen a thousand times -> logos hide things on purpose (bear) -> so do monuments (chains)
> -> and their skeletons (Eiffel's frame) -> which links to Eiffel's own tower -> a monument that shows its own
> history (colour line) -> a hidden recipe (Pantheon) -> a hidden fix (pennies) -> a hidden shape that fights the
> wind -> back to the arrow (loop).
> Draft length: 405 words, about 2:25 at the chess Short's pace (445 words = 2:42). Not yet measured with TTS.

## S00 · The arrow
@scene S00_Arrow
@lead 0.4
There's an arrow on the side of every FedEx truck, and once you spot it in the white gap between the E and the x, you'll never be able to unsee it.
And its designer says he found it by accident while he was squeezing the letters together, but then he kept it, because the best secrets are the ones hiding in plain sight.

## S01 · The bear
@scene S01_Bear
So look again at a Toblerone box, and in the white of the mountain, there's a bear standing on its back legs, because the chocolate comes from Bern, a city with a bear on its flag.

## S02 · The chains
@scene S02_Chains
And it isn't just logos, because the Statue of Liberty is stepping out of a broken chain, and you can't see it from the ground, since it's tucked under her robe at her feet.
Then inside her, there's a whole second secret, because her copper skin is only about as thin as two coins, so it hangs on an iron skeleton built by Gustave Eiffel.

## S03 · The lean
@scene S03_Lean
And Eiffel's own tower hides something too, because on a sunny day, the side facing the sun warms up and stretches, so the top quietly leans away from it.

## S04 · The line
@scene S04_Line
Meanwhile, the Washington Monument changes colour about a third of the way up, because the money ran out and work stopped for more than twenty years, and when it restarted, the old quarry's marble didn't match anymore.

## S05 · The recipe
@scene S05_Recipe
But some secrets are in the material itself, because the Romans built the Pantheon's dome with heavy stone at the bottom and lighter and lighter volcanic rock toward the top.
And the square hollows and the open hole in the roof take out even more weight, which is why, after nearly nineteen hundred years, it's still the biggest unreinforced concrete dome on Earth.

## S06 · The pennies
@scene S06_Pennies
And in London, the giant clock under Big Ben is still fine-tuned with a stack of old pennies on its pendulum, where every coin added makes it gain less than half a second a day.

## S07 · The wind
@scene S07_Wind
And even the tallest building in the world has a hidden shape, because from above, the Burj Khalifa is a Y, and its wings step back in a spiral as it rises, which engineers describe as confusing the wind, so it can't build up the swirls that would rock the tower.

## S08 · Back to the arrow
@scene S08_Loop
So the next time you walk past something famous, look twice, because the clue might be right in front of you, just like that arrow.
