# Master prompt for a new "Cut-away" Short

## In a new chat on this repo (short version)

Claude reads `CLAUDE.md` automatically in every new chat on this repo, so this is enough:

```
Make a new Cut-away Short. Follow CLAUDE.md: give me 4 topic ideas first.
```

To pick the topic yourself: `Make a new Cut-away Short about <topic>. Follow CLAUDE.md.`
To change a finished one: `In shrimp_short, <what to change>. Then re-render and send me the new video.`

## Anywhere else (full version, paste all of it)

```
You are my video producer. Make one vertical 3D-animated "how it works" Short for YouTube Shorts and
Instagram Reels, in the same genre as Zack D. Films (one surprising fact, 30-40 seconds), but in our own
look with our own characters and script. Never copy another channel's characters, style, script or title.

Steps (stop and wait for my OK where it says STOP):
1. Topic: give me 4 ideas. For each: the hook sentence, the mechanism in one line, what we would show,
   and mark one "(Recommended)". Pick solid, surprising science. Check that Zack D. Films hasn't already
   made it, and tell me how sure that check is.
2. Research: find the primary sources. List every claim with its source, and flag anything contested or
   estimated. The script must not claim more than the sources do.
3. Script, STOP: about 95-100 words, 4-5 beats, one scene per beat, about 35 seconds. The hook lands in the
   first sentence. Every sentence starts with a connective or an explicit link to the one before. No
   fragments, colon reveals or mic-drop kickers. Show it to me as a table: scene, narration, what you see.
   Wait for "approved".
4. Build the animation in our "Cut-away" look:
   - 1080 x 1920, 60 fps; everything important inside the middle 3:4 (y 240-1680); word captions in the
     band y 1330-1490.
   - The real thing first, then a clean slice or block cut out of it, lit like a specimen.
   - Labels: Inter ExtraBold, short, uppercase, on dark pills. Cinnabar #E0502F is the one accent colour
     (the spoken word in the captions and the one label that matters per scene).
   - One concept = one colour in every scene; hedges shown on screen too (STILL DEBATED, ESTIMATED).
   - The last frame equals the first frame, so the Short loops.
   - Voice: Kokoro af_heart at 0.95. A light marimba music bed. Only self-made sound effects.
   Check stills of the key moments before rendering everything.
5. Render, mix (about -14 LUFS), burn in word captions, and check: captions match the script, the loop
   seam is invisible. Make a cover (a strong frame plus a 3-line title, 3:4-safe) and upload notes
   (3 titles, description with sources, hashtags, pinned comment).
6. STOP: send me the video and the cover with a short summary. I'll ask for changes as v2.

Keep your messages to me short and simple.
```
